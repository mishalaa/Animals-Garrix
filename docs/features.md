# Features (Phase 1)

## Getting started

- Welcome screen with English / বাংলা switch
- Sign up and sign in (with Supabase)
- Demo mode with three sample pets, no setup needed

## Home

- Greeting and pet search
- "Due today" card with task and overdue counts
- Filter pets by species
- Pet cards with a marker when a pet needs attention

## Pet profile

- Photo, name, species, breed, Age / Sex / Weight tiles
- Allergies always shown in red at the top
- Tabs: Overview, History, Visits, Medicines, Reports, Origin
- Edit or delete a pet

## Identity and origin

- Species: cat, dog, bird, rabbit, other
- Breed list including "Local / Deshi" and "Mixed", with mix details
- Breed health warnings (e.g. Persian: kidney, breathing, heat)
- Sex, birth date (with "estimated"), weight, spayed / neutered
- Coat type, colour / pattern, microchip, distinguishing marks
- How the pet joined: street rescue, shelter, breeder, pet shop, gift, born at home
- Where found, date joined, condition when found, rescuer or shelter, their story

## Medical history

- Illnesses, chronic conditions, injuries, surgeries
- Status (active, managed, resolved), severity, diagnosis and resolved dates
- Symptoms, treatment, diagnosing vet, "before adoption" flag
- Allergies by type with reaction

## Vet visits and medicines

- Visits: date, clinic, vet, reason, diagnosis, notes, cost in BDT
- Link a visit to an illness and mark it resolved from the visit
- Go straight to adding the medicine the vet prescribed
- Medicines, vaccines, deworming, flea and tick, supplements
- Frequency, times of day, start / end dates, course length, next due date
- Course progress (e.g. "12 of 120 doses given")

## Schedule and reminders

- 7-day week strip
- Today's doses with Done / Skip / Undo
- Overdue vaccines and deworming carried over
- Marking a vaccine done moves its next due date forward
- Phone reminders on Android and iOS

## Reports

- Upload photos or PDFs: lab, x-ray, prescription, vaccine card, invoice
- Link to a visit or illness; open later

## Vet summary

- One page: allergies, active illnesses, current medicines, vaccines, recent visits, past illnesses
- Print or save as PDF (web); share PDF (phone)

## Sharing and settings

- Share a pet by email as editor or viewer; remove access
- Language switch with Bangla dates and numbers
- Reset demo data, sign out

## Security

- Records visible only to a pet's owner and people it's shared with
- Private file storage; 17 automated permission tests
